BogoCam Support

A native-style camera that lets you flip between the front and back cameras in the middle of a video — one continuous take.

How it works

  • Flip while recording — tap the flip button mid-recording and the camera swaps front↔back in one continuous clip, with a 3-D flip baked right into the video.
  • Dual camera (PiP) — tap the PiP button (next to the flash) to record both cameras at once, with the second camera in a floating inset. Drag the inset to move it, flick it to a corner, and pinch it to resize. Supported on devices with dual-camera capture.
  • Photo & Video — swipe or tap PHOTO / VIDEO. Shoot in 4:3, 16:9, or 1:1, up to 4K, at 24/30/60/120 fps.
  • Zoom — pinch, tap a lens (0.5× / 1× / 2×), or drag the rotary zoom dial.
  • Everything else — flash/torch, self-timer, grid, tap-to-focus and exposure, and a built-in viewer: tap the corner thumbnail to swipe through your library, swipe up for photo info, swipe down to return to the camera.
  • Landscape — turn the phone and your captures are saved in the right orientation.

Troubleshooting

  • Black preview / "camera access needed" — allow Camera access in iOS Settings → BogoCam. Microphone access is needed to record audio with video.
  • Captures aren't saving / the thumbnail is empty — allow Photos access in iOS Settings → BogoCam so the app can save and show your captures.
  • No location on the map in Photos — allow Location access ("While Using the App") in iOS Settings → BogoCam, and give the phone a moment to get a GPS fix before capturing.
  • PiP / dual camera unavailable — simultaneous dual-camera capture needs a supported device; on older devices the PiP button will say it isn't available.
  • The screen looks dim during a long recording — this is iOS reducing brightness to manage heat during extended high-resolution recording, the same as the built-in Camera.
