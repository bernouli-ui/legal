Chess Guru Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Summary

Chess Guru ("the App") does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, usage analytics, or device information. The chess engine, the coach and the chat all run on your device. The App has no backend server, no account system, and no analytics.

What information is collected

None. Chess Guru does not have a backend server, account system, login, or analytics, and makes no network connections of its own.

What information is shared with third parties

None. No third-party SDKs, advertisers, analytics providers, or data brokers are integrated.

Asking the coach

Questions you type in "Ask the coach" are answered on your device, by the chess engine built into the App and, on devices with Apple Intelligence, by Apple's on-device language model. The App does not send your questions anywhere.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The App never sees your payment details; it only receives Apple's confirmation that the purchase was made, so it can unlock the full version.

Local data

Chess Guru stores the following on your device only:

  • The games you review or play (their moves, the player names in them, and the engine's analysis), which make up your game history.
  • The player names you've told the App are yours, so your pieces are shown at the bottom of the board.
  • Your settings, such as the board theme, piece set and analysis strength.
  • A list of the games that used one of the free games, kept in the device's Keychain as fingerprints of the moves, so the count isn't reset by reinstalling.

Your game history and settings are removed when you uninstall the App. None of this is ever transmitted off your device. Exporting a game as PGN is your own action, and only when you choose it.

Children's privacy

Because the App collects no data of any kind, it complies with COPPA and similar regulations by design.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
