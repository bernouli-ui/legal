Chess Guru Support

A chess coach you can ask why: review your games move by move, play the engine with every move graded, and ask the coach about any position. Everything runs on your device.

How it works

  • Review a game — copy a game's PGN from chess.com, lichess or any chess app, then tap Review a Game and paste it. Every move is classified, from Brilliant to Blunder, with the coach's explanation.
  • Play — play the engine from the starting position at five levels. Each move you play is graded straight away. Hint shows which piece to move, then the move; Undo takes your move back.
  • Ask the coach — tap Ask during a review or a game. Suggested questions are answered by the engine on any device; answers that are about moves play out on a small board.
  • Skills — lessons and puzzles for 41 skills, with progress counted across your own games.

Questions

  • How do I get a game's PGN? — on chess.com, open the game, tap Share, then PGN, and copy it. On lichess, open the game, tap the menu, then Share & export, then PGN. Most chess apps have a similar "Share" or "Export" option.
  • Typed questions get short answers — answering questions in your own words uses Apple Intelligence, which needs iOS 26 or later on a supported iPhone or iPad with Apple Intelligence turned on (Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri). Without it, the engine answers with the closest fit. The suggested questions work on every device.
  • What's free? — 10 games: games you paste to review, or games against the engine. Games already in your history always open and re-analyze for free, and the built-in sample game never counts. After that, one purchase unlocks unlimited reviews and games. There's no subscription.
  • I bought it on another device — open Settings in the App and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account. Family Sharing is supported.
  • The board is upside down — the App puts your pieces at the bottom. Add your name as it appears in your games under Settings → Your player names, or tap the other player on the review's overview.
  • Analysis is slow — choose Fast in Settings → Analysis. Deep looks further ahead and takes longer.
