Forte Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Summary

Forte ("the App") does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, usage analytics, or device information. The App has no backend server, no account system, and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What information is collected

None. Forte does not have a backend server, account system, login, or analytics.

What information is shared with third parties

None. No third-party SDKs, advertisers, analytics providers, or data brokers are integrated. The App makes no network connections.

Local data

Forte stores the following on your device only:

  • Your practice history: the days you completed the daily challenge, your streak, and today's progress.
  • The lessons you have completed, your XP, level, and accuracy in each practice area.
  • Your app preferences (sound and haptics).

This data is saved in a local file in the App's own storage and is removed when you uninstall the App. It is never transmitted off your device.

So that the Forte widget can show your streak, the App also keeps a small copy of your completed days in storage shared only with its own widget on the same device.

Children's privacy

Forte is rated 4+. Because the App collects no data of any kind, it complies with COPPA and similar regulations by design.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
