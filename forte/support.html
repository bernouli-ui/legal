Forte Support

Daily ear training, sight reading and music theory practice, with a streak to keep you going.

How it works

  • Daily challenge — three short sections each day: ear training, sight reading and music theory. Finish all three to complete the day.
  • Streak and widget — every completed day adds to your streak. Add the Forte widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen: it turns gray until you've practiced today.
  • Lessons — 129 lessons in 13 units, from reading the staff to harmony, form and modes. Each has key points and a five-question quiz; four right answers complete it.
  • Practice — run any drill on its own (intervals, chords, note reading, key signatures, interval spelling, scale degrees) as often as you like.
  • Progress and badges — XP, levels, accuracy in each area, and 69 badges to earn.

Troubleshooting

  • I can't hear the ear-training sounds — turn the volume up, and check that Sound is on in Forte's Settings tab.
  • My streak went back to zero — a streak counts calendar days with all three sections finished. Missing a whole day starts a new streak; your best streak is kept.
  • The widget shows an old streak — open Forte once. iOS refreshes widgets on its own schedule, and the app updates the widget every time you finish a section.
  • Will an update erase my progress? — no. Your progress stays on your iPhone and is kept across updates. Deleting the app removes it.
