Mahjong Points Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Summary

Mahjong Points ("the App") does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, usage analytics, or device information. The App has no backend server, no account system, and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What information is collected

None. Mahjong Points does not have a backend server, account system, login, or analytics.

What information is shared with third parties

None. No third-party SDKs, advertisers, analytics providers, or data brokers are integrated. The App makes no network connections.

Local data

Mahjong Points stores the following on your device only:

  • The players, seasons, and recorded hands (including the tiles of each win) that make up your scoreboard.
  • Your app preferences, such as the chosen language.

This data is saved in a local file in the App's own storage and is removed when you uninstall the App. It is never transmitted off your device.

Children's privacy

Mahjong Points is rated 4+. Because the App collects no data of any kind, it complies with COPPA and similar regulations by design.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
