Zero Incidents Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Summary

Zero Incidents ("the App") does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, usage analytics, or device information. The App has no backend server, no account system, and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your device. Optional tips are purchases processed by Apple.

What information is collected

None. Zero Incidents does not have a backend server, account system, login, or analytics.

What information is shared with third parties

None. No third-party SDKs, advertisers, analytics providers, or data brokers are integrated. The App makes no network connections of its own. If you leave a tip, the purchase goes through Apple's App Store (see Tips below).

Local data

Zero Incidents stores the following on your device only:

  • Your incident log: each incident's date and time, type, severity, statement, and whether and when it was resolved, with your settlement notes.
  • The date the safety sign counts from, and your app preferences (such as whether the coach may read a summary of your log).

This data is saved in one local file (state.json) in storage that only the App and its own widget can use on your device (an App Group container). The widget reads the file to show your count; it never changes it. The file is removed when you uninstall the App. It is never transmitted off your device, and there is no copy anywhere else.

The coach

The Safety Officer coach runs on Apple's on-device language model (Apple Intelligence), on your iPhone or iPad. What you type, the coach's replies and, if you allow it in Settings, the short summary of your log it can read are processed on your device. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Chats are not saved by the App: starting a new chat or closing the App clears them.

Hotline links

The App's Safety page has buttons to call or text emergency services, the National Domestic Violence Hotline and the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, and a link to thehotline.org. They open the Phone, Messages or Safari app only when you tap them. The App doesn't record that you tapped them. Calls and texts show up in your phone's own history, as any call does.

Tips

Zero Incidents is free. In Settings you can leave an optional tip, an in-app purchase that doesn't unlock anything. Tips are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store:

  • We never see or receive your payment details, your Apple Account, your name or your email.
  • The only information we get about tips is Apple's anonymous sales reports: how many tips were bought, with no way to tell who bought them.
  • The App doesn't save anything about your tips, and sends nothing about them anywhere.

Apple's handling of the purchase is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Zero Incidents is rated 9+ and is meant for adults in a relationship. Because the App collects no data of any kind, it complies with COPPA and similar regulations by design.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
